Tea worth taking your time over

Kettle & Leaf started as a weekend stall at the farmers' market and grew into a little shop with a big tasting bar. We stock fifteen teas and tools at a time, chosen by tasting hundreds of samples a season. If it's on our shelf, it's something we brew at home.

  • Direct trade We buy straight from farms and co-ops we've visited.
  • Packed fresh Tins are filled by hand in small weekly batches.
  • Brewing help Every order ships with a steeping guide card.
