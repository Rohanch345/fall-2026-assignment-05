About us

From one market stall to a neighborhood tasting bar

Our mission

To make exceptional tea feel approachable by sourcing it honestly, packing it fresh, and teaching anyone who asks how to brew it well.

Our story

Kettle & Leaf began in 2018 with a folding table, two kettles and a dozen tins at the Portland Saturday Market. Our founders had spent years traveling through tea regions in Japan, Taiwan and India, and wanted to share the teas they couldn't find at home.

The stall's regulars kept asking for more, so in 2021 we opened a small shop with a six-seat tasting bar. We still work with many of the same growers we met on those first trips, and we still taste every lot before it reaches the shelf.

We keep our catalog small on purpose. Fifteen products means we can know each one well and help you find the one you'll love.

What we value

  • Fair sourcing

    We pay growers above market rate and publish where every tea comes from.

  • Freshness

    Small, frequent orders mean nothing sits on our shelf long.

  • Low waste

    Our tins are refillable. Bring one back for 10% off a refill.
